# Monday 23 November 2026: 38 dated stories, 9 regions — COP31 fails, Houthi strikes Riyadh, Mexico captures 'El Chapo Isidro'

A one-day newspaper front page summing up the world's news of Monday
23 November 2026: 38 date-checked stories across 9 regions — COP31
failing in Türkiye after its final draft dropped "fossil fuels", Houthi
drones and missiles striking Riyadh for a third straight day, and Mexico
capturing the FBI's most-wanted cartel boss "El Chapo Isidro" — plus a
numbered world map, a UTC day strip, and a date check that keeps earlier
days' news out.

The three hero stories get full cards with original drawn panels (the
COP31 globe and thermometer, the Riyadh night skyline with interception
bursts, the FBI badge and handcuffs), and the remaining 35 stories are
listed by region and pinned on the map. Every story is dated 23 November
2026 and its source is named in the footer.

Competition: `news-image-20261123` — "Sum up 23 November's world news in
one image" ($100, make.fun).

## Run

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

Writes `out/entry.png` (1024x1024 PNG, Python standard library plus
Pillow only, no network, deterministic: two runs write identical bytes).
